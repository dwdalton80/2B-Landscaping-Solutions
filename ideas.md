**Idea A — "Oklahoma Craftsman"**

**Design Movement:** American Craftsman meets contemporary outdoor living — rugged, honest, and grounded.

**Core Principles:**
- Earthy warmth with natural material textures (stone, wood grain, soil)
- Strong asymmetric layouts that feel like a well-laid stone path
- Typography that feels hand-crafted but professional
- Photography-forward with full-bleed imagery

**Color Philosophy:** Deep forest green (#1a3a2a) as primary, warm amber/tan (#c8a96e) as accent, off-white parchment (#f5f0e8) for backgrounds. Evokes the Oklahoma landscape — rich soil, lush grass, warm sun.

**Layout Paradigm:** Diagonal section dividers (clip-path) creating a sense of terrain. Offset card grids where service cards are staggered like stepping stones. Hero is full-viewport with a strong diagonal text overlay.

**Signature Elements:**
- Diagonal section transitions (like a sloped hillside)
- Stone-texture background panels
- Leaf/branch SVG motifs as dividers

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover states reveal earthy overlays on service cards. Scroll-triggered entrance animations bring content in from the sides like growing plants.

**Animation:** Subtle fade-up on scroll for content blocks. Hero text slides in from left. Service cards scale up slightly on hover with a green overlay.

**Typography System:** "Playfair Display" for headings (elegant serif with character), "Source Sans 3" for body (clean, readable). Heading hierarchy uses weight contrast — bold display vs. regular body.

**Idea B — "Bold Industrial Green"**

**Design Movement:** Industrial modernism meets landscape — bold, confident, no-nonsense.

**Core Principles:**
- High contrast dark/light sections
- Oversized typography as a design element
- Grid-breaking layouts with strong horizontal bands
- Minimal decoration — let the work speak

**Color Philosophy:** Near-black charcoal (#1c1c1e) sections alternating with pure white, with a vivid grass green (#4a8c3f) as the sole accent. Creates a striking, memorable identity.

**Layout Paradigm:** Alternating full-width dark and light bands. Service section uses a large horizontal scroll or masonry grid. Numbers and stats displayed in oversized type.

**Signature Elements:**
- Oversized service numbers (01, 02, 03...)
- Full-bleed photo sections with minimal text overlay
- Thick green horizontal rule/dividers

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover on services reveals a full-color photo. Navigation is minimal — sticky top bar that becomes solid on scroll.

**Animation:** Staggered number count-up animations. Photos fade in with a slight zoom. Section transitions use a green line sweep.

**Typography System:** "Barlow Condensed" for headings (industrial, strong), "DM Sans" for body. Heavy weight contrast between display and body text.

**Idea C — "Warm Southern Roots"**

**Design Movement:** Southern hospitality meets professional landscape craft — approachable, warm, community-focused.

**Core Principles:**
- Warm, inviting color palette evoking Oklahoma summers
- Organic shapes and rounded forms (not rigid grids)
- Personal, community-focused messaging
- Photography integrated naturally into layout

**Color Philosophy:** Sage green (#7a9e7e) as primary, warm cream (#fdf6ec) as background, terracotta (#c4622d) as accent. Feels like a front porch in Durant — welcoming and authentic.

**Layout Paradigm:** Flowing, organic sections with gentle curves (SVG wave dividers). Services displayed in a warm card layout with rounded corners. About section uses a split layout with a personal photo feel.

**Signature Elements:**
- Wave/curve SVG section dividers
- Warm card shadows (terracotta-tinted)
- Handwritten-style accent font for quotes/taglines

**Interaction Philosophy:** Gentle, smooth transitions. Cards lift softly on hover. Contact form is prominent and friendly.

**Animation:** Gentle fade-in on scroll. Wave dividers animate subtly. CTA buttons have a warm pulse effect.

**Typography System:** "Lora" for headings (warm serif), "Nunito" for body (friendly, rounded). Taglines use "Dancing Script" for a personal touch.

## Selected Approach: **Idea A — "Oklahoma Craftsman"**

This design best reflects 2B Landscaping's identity as a skilled, locally rooted craftsman business. The earthy palette, diagonal layouts, and photography-forward approach will showcase their work powerfully while feeling authentic to Durant, OK.
